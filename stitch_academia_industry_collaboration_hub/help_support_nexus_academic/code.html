Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a project? expand_more

Navigate to the "Find Projects" tab, select a project that matches your expertise, and click the "Apply" button. Ensure your profile is up to date before applying.

What institutions are supported? expand_more

We support major research universities and accredited academic institutions globally. You can verify your institution during the registration process.

How do I update my profile? expand_more

Go to your Account Settings by clicking your avatar in the top right corner. From there, you can edit your bio, upload documents, and update your research areas.

Still need help?

Our dedicated support team is here to assist you with any complex inquiries or technical issues.
